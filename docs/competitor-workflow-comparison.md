# Competitor contributor-workflow comparison

**Reviewed:** 14 October 2026. This comparison is limited to the official product documentation linked below. It describes documented behavior, not a claim that a capability is present in every plan, deployment, project, media type, or model configuration. “Not documented” means the reviewed sources did not establish the behavior; it is not proof that a product never supports it.

## Executive summary

- **Undo/redo is an editor action, not a revision archive.** All five products document editor undo/redo, generally with Ctrl/Cmd+Z and a redo shortcut. The reviewed pages do not establish that an undo stack survives leaving an item, refreshing, submitting, or reopening a session. Durable history must be evaluated separately.
- **Autosave/recovery differs sharply.** Label Studio most directly documents automatic saves and unfinished drafts. CVAT documents optional autosave, off by default with a 15-minute default interval, plus explicit Save/Finish actions, but not a general recovery guarantee. Labelbox documents explicit save/submit. General autosave/recovery was not established in the reviewed Supervisely or Roboflow material.
- **“History” means different things.** Roboflow documents per-image annotation history and revert. Supervisely documents named project versions, comparisons and restore-by-new-project. Labelbox documents data-row and workflow history. Label Studio documents an editor history/draft panel with edition qualifications. CVAT documents a narrower editor annotation history for supported selection/batch actions and advertises Enterprise audit logs; the reviewed material does not establish a general annotation revision browser.
- **Pixel masks are first-class in the documented image workflows.** Brush, eraser, pixel sizing and mask operations appear across the five products, although model-generated masks, mask interpolation, media support and availability vary.
- **Video object tracks are not universal.** CVAT, Labelbox, Supervisely and Label Studio document keyframes/interpolation or tracking in specified workflows. Roboflow’s reviewed documentation establishes temporal video segments, not object-keyframe tracking. Several products restrict model tracking or video masks by model, plan, application or editor.
- **Human review is a common workflow, but not a universal AI gate.** Prediction review/correction is documented broadly; Label Studio documents an Auto accept option, and Roboflow’s approve/reject gate is configurable and plan-dependent. Documentation does not support claiming that every competitor technically blocks every prediction until a human approves it.

## Product-specific notes and evidence

### CVAT

CVAT documents an optional editor auto-save setting (off by default; 15-minute default interval), explicit Save work/Finish job, configurable shortcut scopes and hover hints, and an editor annotation history for supported multi-object-selection/batch operations. Its brush guide covers pixel masks, adjustable brush size, erasing and polygon-to-mask operations. Track mode documents manual video tracks, keyframes, interpolation, keyframe navigation and Outside state. Automatic annotation and AI tools require an available model; documented Review mode and QA workflows should not be mistaken for an unconditional technical acceptance gate.

Official sources: [Editor controls](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/annotation-editor/navbar/), [auto-save settings](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/annotation-editor/settings/), [shortcuts](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/getting_started/shortcuts/), [annotation history scope](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/manual-annotation/utilities/multi-object-selection/), [brush masks](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/manual-annotation/shapes/annotation-with-brush-tool/), [manual track mode](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/manual-annotation/modes/track-mode-basics/), [automatic annotation](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/auto-annotation/automatic-annotation/), [AI tools](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/annotation/auto-annotation/ai-tools/), [manual QA](https://docs.cvat.ai/docs/qa-analytics/manual-qa/).

### Labelbox

Labelbox documents current-asset undo/redo, an explicit shortcut reference, per-data-row and workflow history, image/video mask tools, video keyframes and interpolation for boxes/polylines/points, plus model-assisted labeling. AI bounding-box tracking is documented; masks are a video-frame-local exception in the reviewed guide. Brush availability can depend on ontology age; model assistance depends on a compatible project/model workflow.

Official sources: [keyboard shortcuts](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/guides/keyboard-shortcuts), [release notes on undo scope](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/release-notes), [image editor](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/guides/image-editor), [video editor](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/guides/video-editor), [model-assisted labeling](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/guides/foundry-model-assisted-labeling), [Catalog annotation history](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/guides/explore-your-data-in-catalog), [workflow history](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/guides/workflows), [saving data rows](https://docs.labelbox.com/horizon/guides/data-rows-activity).

### Supervisely

Supervisely distinguishes editor Undo/Redo and Restore mode from named project versions and comparison. The image brush guide documents mask modes and pixel editing. Videos 3.0 documents keyframes, interpolation, timelines/trajectories and app/model-backed Auto Tracking. Live Training prediction review is explicit in that workflow, while its guide qualifies availability by Enterprise license extension, GPU and software version; other trackers depend on installed apps/models.

Official sources: [image labeling toolbox](https://docs.supervisely.com/labeling/labeling-toolbox/images), [video labeling toolbox](https://docs.supervisely.com/labeling/labeling-toolbox/videos-3.0), [project versions](https://docs.supervisely.com/data-organization/project-dataset/project-versions), [Live Training](https://docs.supervisely.com/labeling/overview/live-training), [brush tool](https://docs.supervisely.com/labeling/labeling-tools/brush-tool), [quality control](https://docs.supervisely.com/labeling/jobs/labeling-quality-control).

### Label Studio

Label Studio is the clearest reviewed source for automatically saved edits and unfinished drafts, while still leaving retention and crash guarantees unspecified. It documents discoverable/customizable hotkeys, BrushLabels RLE masks and BitmaskLabels pixel masks. Interactive model predictions support human accept/reject when Auto accept is disabled; Auto accept is an explicit alternative. VideoVector documents keyframes and linear interpolation, while SAM2 propagation and the comparison matrix qualify feature/plan availability.

Official sources: [labeling guide](https://labelstud.io/guide/labeling), [hotkeys](https://labelstud.io/guide/hotkeys), [task JSON and drafts](https://labelstud.io/guide/task_format), [ML predictions](https://labelstud.io/guide/ml), [prediction behavior](https://labelstud.io/guide/predictions), [BrushLabels](https://labelstud.io/tags/brushlabels), [BitmaskLabels](https://labelstud.io/tags/bitmasklabels), [VideoVector](https://labelstud.io/tags/videovector), [edition comparison](https://labelstud.io/guide/label_studio_compare).

### Roboflow

Roboflow explicitly documents per-image annotation history and reverting to an earlier version, plus editor undo/redo and pixel-level brush masks. Its Auto Label guidance recommends human quality review; the training guide describes approving correct boxes and correcting/removing incorrect ones. Review Mode can impose approval before inclusion only when the workspace/plan workflow enables it. The reviewed docs establish video segments, not object keyframe tracking or interpolation.

Official sources: [Annotate editor](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/use-roboflow-annotate), [keyboard shortcuts](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/use-roboflow-annotate/keyboard-shortcuts), [annotation history and revert](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/use-roboflow-annotate/annotation-history), [team collaboration/Review Mode](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/team-collaboration), [Label Assist](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/ai-labeling/model-assisted-labeling), [Auto Label](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/ai-labeling/auto-label), [annotation tools](https://docs.roboflow.com/datasets/annotate/annotate/annotation-tools).

## Implementation implications for GeoLabel

1. Define undo as a bounded editor-local action stack; do not present it as permanent history.
2. Persist unsent drafts separately from final submissions, display save state/time, expose recovery/discard, and use version checks to surface cross-tab conflicts rather than silently overwriting.
3. Make shortcuts discoverable in the editor and pause them while a user types into a field; retain equivalent visible buttons.
4. Keep submitted revision history immutable and distinct from autosaved drafts, undo/redo and operational audit records. A restore action should copy a past version into a new draft, not rewrite the audit trail.
5. Keep AI suggestions as provider-attributed candidates requiring explicit human review in GeoLabel. Do not assume competitors’ configurable workflows imply a universal acceptance gate, and do not enable external inference without provider, data-transfer and spend authorization.
6. Store masks with explicit pixel dimensions/encoding and preserve the task CRS/GeoJSON boundary geometry. Video tracks should identify keyframes and interpolation semantics; do not interpolate masks unless the representation and behavior are deliberately defined.
